About Me

I'm a Master of Computer Science student at UC Irvine who loves turning ideas into working software and AI systems. My recent work in the AIEA Lab focuses on computer vision for autonomous driving, where I build and evaluate models for scene reconstruction with Gaussian splatting and object detection in busy city streets.

I enjoy end-to-end work: collecting and cleaning data, training and testing models, and shipping simple interfaces so others can use the results. Teammates know me as calm under pressure, clear in communication, and quick to learn new tools. I care about clean code, solid experiments, and useful demos.

I'm excited to keep building ML products that are reliable, explainable, and helpful in the real world. When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing Monster Hunter or exploring new technologies and frameworks.
